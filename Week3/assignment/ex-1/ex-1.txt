1. What columns violate 1NF?

Answer: food_description and food_code columns has multiple values, that's 1NF violation.

2. What entities do you recognize that could be extracted?

Answer: member, dinner, venue, food.

3. Name all the tables and columns that would make a 3NF compliant solution.

Answer: 

--> Members table: member_id (primary key), member_name, member_adress;
--> Dinners table: dinner_id (primary key), dinner_date, venue_code;
--> Venues table: venue_code (primary key), venue_description;
--> Food table: food_code (primary key), food_description;

+ Two combined tables:
--> Dinner-Food table as we have many-to-many relationship between dinner and food;
--> Member-Dinner tble as we have many-to-many relationship between member and dinner;

** Dinner-Food table: dinner_id, food_id
** Member-Dinner table: member_id, dinner_id
